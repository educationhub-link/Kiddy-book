🧠 Adolescent Psychosocial Screening (HEADSS)

Quick Reference

Growing up can be challenging, and it is common to face challenges in our lives. This questionnaire helps healthcare professionals understand these challenges in order to better support the adolescent.

There is no right or wrong answer to these questions.

Information shared should be kept private between the adolescent and the healthcare professional, except in situations where the adolescent or someone else may be at risk of getting hurt.
