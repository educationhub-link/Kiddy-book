🍼 Formula Calculation

Quick Reference

FORMULA
Weight in kg × 150 mL Number of feeds in a day = mL per feed

⭐ Worked Example

👶 Baby weight: 4 kg
🍼 Number of feeds: 8 feeds/day
Step 1:
4 kg × 150 mL = 600 mL/day
Step 2:
600 mL ÷ 8 feeds = 75 mL/feed
✓ 75 mL per feed
