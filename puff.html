💨 Puff Calculation

Quick Reference

FORMULA
0.3 × Weight (kg) = Number of Puffs
Round the calculated result to the nearest whole puff.

👦 Child C

Sex: Male
Age: 1 year 11 months
Weight: 10.5 kg
Diagnosis: Bronchiolitis
Dr ordered Salbutamol 100 mcg inhaler, inhalation every 4 hourly.

How many puffs should be given?
0.3 × 10.5 = 3.15 puffs
→ Rounded answer: 3 puffs
