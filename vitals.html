💚 VITAL PARAMETERS
Hypotension is defined as systolic BP:
Neonate < 60 mmHg
Infants (1–12 mths) < 70 mmHg
Children (1–10 yrs) < 70 mmHg + [2 × (Age in Yrs)]
Children > 10 yrs < 90 mmHg
MAP = 50 + [2 × (Age in Years)] mmHg
